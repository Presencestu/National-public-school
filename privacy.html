Information we collect

When you use the admissions enquiry form on this website, we collect the information you choose to provide, which may include your name, phone number, the class you are enquiring about, and any message you write. We do not require you to create an account or provide payment information through this website.

Standard technical information — such as browser type, device type, and pages visited — may also be collected automatically as part of ordinary website operation and analytics.

How we use information

  • To respond to admissions enquiries and contact you about your query.
  • To maintain records related to the admissions process.
  • To understand how the website is used, so it can be improved.
  • To meet legal, regulatory, or safeguarding obligations where applicable.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use enquiry information for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Cookies

This website may use essential cookies required for basic functionality, and may use analytics cookies to understand aggregate visitor behaviour. Cookies do not contain your enquiry form content. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; doing so may affect some website functionality.

Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and displays a map of the school's location using Google Maps. These services may process limited technical data, such as your IP address, in accordance with their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review those policies directly.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for use by parents, guardians, and staff for admissions and general information purposes. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through this website. Any information about a student submitted through the enquiry form is provided by a parent or guardian and is used solely for admissions purposes.

Data retention

Enquiry information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to process the admissions enquiry and maintain appropriate school records, currently set at [Add retention period]. Information is deleted or anonymised when it is no longer needed for these purposes.

Data security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

You may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, request that inaccurate information be corrected, or request that your information be deleted where it is no longer needed. To make such a request, please contact the school using the details below.

Changes to this policy
