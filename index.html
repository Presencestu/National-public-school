02 — The School

A school built around its students, not around a syllabus alone.

National Public School was founded on a simple conviction: that children learn best when they are known well. Class sizes are kept deliberately manageable, teachers stay closely involved in each student's progress, and the school day is structured to make room for both rigour and rest. Academics are taken seriously here — but never at the cost of the person doing the learning.

The school serves families across Lami Chour and the surrounding Sumeri Chhapar community, and is led day-to-day by Principal Mr. Ramjanam Singh, whose approach to school life anchors the culture across every classroom.

03 — Philosophy

What we believe about learning.

  • Attention over volume

    A child who is noticed learns differently to one who is processed. Every teacher at NPS is expected to know the students in front of them — their pace, their gaps, their strengths — not just the syllabus they are teaching.

  • Character alongside curriculum

    Grades measure a narrow slice of a child's ability. Discipline, honesty, effort and respect for others are treated as outcomes of equal weight, taught deliberately rather than assumed.

  • Learning that is seen, not just tested

    Understanding shows up in how a student explains an idea, builds something, or asks a better question — not only in an answer sheet. Classrooms are run to make that thinking visible.

04 — Academics

How students grow, stage by stage.

The academic programme is organised in three stages, each designed around what students at that age genuinely need — not a scaled-down version of the stage above it.

Foundational

Early years focused on language, number sense, motor skills and the habits of a classroom — curiosity first, structure close behind.

Middle Years

Subject depth increases alongside independence — students begin to plan their own work, ask harder questions, and take ownership of outcomes.

Senior Secondary

Rigorous preparation for board examinations paired with real guidance on subject choices, aptitude and what comes after school.

07 — Why National Public School

What sets a school like this apart.

  • Class sizes that allow real attention

    A deliberately low student-teacher ratio, so no child is a face in a crowd.

  • Teachers who know each child by name

    Progress is tracked as a person, not a roll number — parents hear specifics, not generalities.

  • A safe, disciplined and caring campus culture

    Structure and warmth are treated as equally important, not opposing forces.

  • A balance of academics, character and activity

    No single measure of a child's development is allowed to crowd out the others.

  • Direct access to the Principal and staff

    Parents and students can reach the people actually running the school, not a call centre.
